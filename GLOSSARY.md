# Food Stuff

Dinner planning, sourced recipes, household stock, and grocery shopping.

## Language

**Sourced recipe**:
A recipe taken from identifiable original material, with a reference that lets the user find that material. A recipe retains its source when selected again or scaled to a different yield.
_Avoid_: Generated recipe

**Individual portion**:
An amount of a prepared meal intended for one person at one dinner.
_Avoid_: Meal when counting servings

**Household dinner**:
One dinner for everyone in the household. A batch can cover several household dinners and leave additional individual portions.

**Stock snapshot**:
Observations and user confirmations about ingredients currently on hand. Observing an ingredient does not establish its remaining usable quantity.
_Avoid_: Inventory when referring to a one-time photo check

**Approved shopping list**:
A particular version of the consolidated ingredients and missing quantities that the user has approved for shopping. Approval belongs to that version rather than to later changes.

**Basket**:
A proposed selection of store products and pack quantities intended to satisfy a shopping list, with its known costs and gaps.

**Cart**:
The product quantities currently held in the grocery service's shopping interface, which can include items unrelated to the approved shopping list.
_Avoid_: Order before checkout
